Questions couples ask us

Still wondering? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person will answer.

What is an AR wedding invitation?
It is a digital wedding invitation that uses augmented reality (AR). When your guest opens the link and taps the button, a cute cartoon version of the couple appears on their floor or table through the phone camera and tells them the wedding date, time and venue.
Do my guests need to install an app?
No. The invitation opens in the phone's browser from a normal link. AR works in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. On a laptop, guests see an animated 3D preview.
How do I send the invitations?
You get a personal link for every guest. Tap the WhatsApp button next to each name and the message with their link is ready to send. You can also share one general link in groups.
Can guests RSVP online?
Yes. Guests reply inside the invitation (attending or not, number of people and a message). You see the replies in your guest list, or they arrive on the couple's WhatsApp.
Which languages and wedding styles are available?
8 languages (English, Sinhala, Tamil, Hindi, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French) and 10 themes: Kandyan, Classic White, Tamil Hindu, Muslim, Tropical Beach, Chinese Red, Indian Royal, Korean Hanbok, Rustic Garden and Royal Night Gala.
How much does a digital wedding invitation cost?
You can start free with 20 invitations. Paid packages start at $5 for 50 invitations, and big weddings with unlimited invitations cost $25. You pay once per wedding.
Can you make the cartoon look like us?
Yes. With the custom couple add-on we draw a cartoon of the bride and groom from your photo, and it appears in your AR invitation.
